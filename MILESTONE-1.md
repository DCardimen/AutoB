# Iteration 1 — Training Glade · Native Pixel v7

The movement prototype now has nine character types rendered as original **64×64 pixel sprites**, with shorter limbs, eight facings, a five-hero four-direction showcase and separate animation examples. It uses Canvas 2D and a crisp shared world grid. The hound stays four-legged; a fox ranger, six-legged brass walker, eight-legged spider and moon jelly expand the representative roster.

Running and sprinting have distance-driven gaits and real acceleration. Cape springs, turn poses, fast braking, physical dodges, constrained death ragdolls, heavy-hit knockdowns and get-up transitions are implemented. Faces now use fixed layouts for each direction, gear draws at the correct depth, and the hound uses flat paws with separate walk, diagonal trot and paired gallop rhythms. The knight and orc alternate overhead/side swings. Every third melee attack charges with a longer windup, greater damage and protection from interruption; incoming damage still applies. Battles reroll six unique creatures per side with melee and ranged options. Shared anatomical and weapon poses retain contact alignment. The Godot export provides **9,792 cells and 136 animations per actor**, with a working Godot 4 demonstration project.
