# V12 relic catalogue

100 new expedition curios. Twenty change the route, economy or capture kit; seventy use combat hooks; ten provide flat stats. Expedition relics affect the team, while captured enemy curios are bound to that individual. Worn curios disappear with their wearer on defeat.

## Examples to try

- Venom Clasp + Serpent Contract + Venom Hourglass: poison enables damage and cooldown recovery.
- Echo Script + Quickglass + Casting Aegis: faster commitments, delayed repeated spells and cast shields. Blood Echo trades health for another repeat.
- Dodge Battery + Evasive Clock + Bramble Footprint: physical dodges feed stamina, cooldowns and nearby roots.
- Overflow Vessel + Cleansing Nectar + Pack Standard: protect a close formation with overhealing shields and cleansing.
- Oracle Lens + Rift Map + Royal Stables: scout recruits, cross selected disconnected edges and stretch the hiring budget.

These are build suggestions, not measured claims of equal power. Enemy relic combinations are inspectable before battle.
