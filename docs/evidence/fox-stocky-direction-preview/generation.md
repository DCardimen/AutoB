# Stocky fox turnaround prompts

Mode: built-in `image_gen`. Three separate calls, one per authored art tier. The previously approved three-tier fox board is the identity/costume/style reference. Output is an art-direction preview, not a rigged sprite atlas or a motion test.

The requested change replaces the coarse 64-pixel preview presentation with larger source artwork and makes the legs shorter and broader, with wide, thick-soled boots. All eight directions are requested for each tier. Right-hand bow ownership remains a fox-specific requirement.

## rowan

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: high-resolution game character turnaround, art-direction preview only. Use the supplied fox archer board as the identity, costume, color and fantasy rendering reference, not as a layout template. Produce a clean 4-column by 2-row turnaround of ONE identical character viewed in E, SE, S, SW across the top row, W, NW, N, NE across the bottom row. E means facing screen-right profile; SE means facing down-right toward viewer in front three-quarter; S full front; SW down-left front three-quarter; W screen-left profile; NW up-left rear three-quarter; N straight rear; NE up-right rear three-quarter. Show genuinely distinct rear diagonal views with backs of heads and cloaks, never repeat front views or just flip a front image. Each full character comfortably inside its own equal spacious panel, same ground baseline, scale, lighting, outfit and stance. Only small readable direction labels beneath each, no other text. Dark muted blue-gray flat background, no scene, no borders, no frame. Preserve detailed fantasy sprite charm with crisp small-scale painted/pixel texture and rich fine material shading, much finer detail and smaller pixel grain than a 64x64 sprite; avoid large stair-step block pixels, blur, photorealism or flat vector cartoon. Characters should be several hundred source pixels tall, not tiny 64-pixel figures enlarged. Most important anatomy revision: unmistakable super-deformed CHIBI physique, about 2.5 heads tall including ears, oversized charming head, compact stout torso, SHORT STOCKY THIGHS AND SHINS, thick legs about 50% broader than the reference, very chunky wide leather boots with broad soles, rounded toes and thick folded cuffs. Both boots firmly planted, broad stable stance; no thin calves, twig legs, long shins, narrow delicate shoes, or skinny ankles. The legs plus boots occupy only the bottom quarter of the body height. Preserve orange fur, cream muzzle/inner ears/tail tips, large dark eyes, green ranger clothes, brown leather gloves, belt and quiver. HOLD THE BOW IN THE ANATOMICAL RIGHT HAND IN ALL EIGHT VIEWS; LEFT HAND relaxed empty. For full front view the bow/right hand is on viewer LEFT, for full rear view bow/right hand is viewer RIGHT. This is a physical right-hand weapon, so maintain identity through the turn; no arbitrary horizontal mirroring. Right fingers visibly wrapped around central bow grip, no floating bow or detached equipment. All arms and legs fully attached, tails and cape connected. Tail, cloak, bow and ears entirely contained in each panel. Relaxed readable idle pose, cape gently draped with slight breeze, no action effects, no skeleton.
FORM: ROWAN base fox ranger, as left column in reference. Simple forest-green tunic and short green capelet, plain brown leather belt/gloves, plain wooden shortbow, one large fluffy orange/cream tail, no crown, no golden ornate armor. Do not age him up or make lanky.
```
